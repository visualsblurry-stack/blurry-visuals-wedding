Phera V — The photographer

Behind the frame

Weddings are the only shoot where nothing repeats. One vidaai, one first look, one grandmother laughing at exactly the right moment — you either caught it or you didn't.

Pheraa is a Mumbai studio built for that pressure. We photograph and film weddings with small, calm crews that know the rituals — when to step close for the mangalsutra and when to disappear entirely. Coverage is planned around light and ceremony order, so families get to be families, not subjects.

Photography and cinema travel together here: stills that look like film frames, films that feel like memory.
