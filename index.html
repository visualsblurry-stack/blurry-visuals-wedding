At Blurry Visuals we believe your wedding is more than a timeline of events — it is an emotional journey. We are here to help you remember how it felt, not just how it looked.

The studio was born in 2024 out of Aakash and Gautam's years behind the lens, capturing love stories in their many forms. What began as a passion gradually became something deeper: a commitment to telling stories that feel real, honest and deeply personal.

Aakash

Approaches every celebration with fresh eyes and an open heart, allowing each narrative to unfold naturally while artfully weaving together the emotions, details and fleeting moments that make every wedding unique.

Gautam

Gravitates toward the quiet, in-between moments — the glance shared when no one is looking, the nervous laughter before the vows, and the subtle magic that often goes unnoticed.

Frequently asked

A little clarity,
before we begin.

Bookings, payment, delivery timelines, revisions, working hours, travel, and everything else worth knowing before you decide.

We photograph weddings in candid, documentary, traditional, portrait, and detail-led styles. Film coverage includes traditional event documentation and story-led cinematic wedding films. Pre-weddings, engagements, haldi, mehndi, sangeet, and receptions can be included in a tailored proposal.
Intimate is ₹1,25,000 and is built for smaller celebrations, with a creative team of four, 1,000–1,200 edited images, a 3–5 minute trailer, and a 10–12 minute wedding movie. Signature is ₹2,25,000 for grand celebrations, with a team of six, 1,500–2,000 edited images, a 15–20 minute movie, a sangeet film, two social reels, and express delivery.
Bookings are confirmed with 50% in advance. A further 40% is due on the first day of the wedding events, and the remaining 10% on delivery of the edited photographs and films.
Editing and delivery begin once 90% of the payment is received. Photographs take approximately eight weeks, as do the cinematic trailer and movie, with some variation during peak season. Traditional video is delivered within three months. Edited content is shared over Google Drive and stays accessible for a year; raw data is delivered on a hard drive you provide and is never uploaded to Drive.
Yes. For photographs, post-production covers colour correction and enhancement to our studio standard; we do not offer extensive manipulation or liquifying. For the wedding movie, two rounds of edits are included in your collection, and further changes are charged at ₹15,000 per revision.
Our team works an acceptable shift of roughly ten hours. Where there is an early event the next morning, we can cover after-parties for up to two hours. Our cut-off on any day is 1:00 AM, beyond which additional coverage is charged at ₹25,000 per hour. The crew and equipment need six to eight hours to recharge and back up between days.
Travel and accommodation for outstation shoots are covered by the client. As the team is on duty throughout, they are treated as guests with access to banqueting and non-alcoholic refreshments. If you would prefer us to arrange meals, let us know before the event; a per-person plate is charged at ₹1,500, ordered a la carte at the hotel or resort.
A pre-wedding shoot is ₹60,000 per day, drone coverage ₹10,000 per day, a coffee table book ₹20,000, raw data ₹20,000, a full event video ₹15,000, and an Instagram reel ₹7,500. Live streaming, behind-the-scenes social coverage, and Instagram handling are available on request.
Traditional videography records the event in a complete, chronological way. Cinematography shapes selected moments, sound, movement, and emotion into a concise story-led film. A collection may include both documentary coverage and a cinematic edit.
